# i18n

## Goal

An app shows its texts in the language of each user: the controllers, the components and the pages of `@nano-app/mantine` read them by key instead of taking them as options.

## Scope

- In `@nano-app/frontend`, not in `@nano-app/mantine`: the controllers, the validation messages of `@nano-app/controllers` and the pages all need it, and none of them depends on Mantine.
- Recommended so far: [next-intl](https://next-intl.dev) behind the framework. `withNanoApp` composes its plugin, `NanoAppProvider` includes `NextIntlClientProvider`, the controllers get `this.t(key)`, the messages live in `src/messages/<locale>.json` and the locale comes from a cookie, with no `/es/...` routes.
- Then the `texts` option of the pages of `@nano-app/mantine` becomes keys of the messages.
- To decide with the maintainer: where the locale comes from (cookie, `Accept-Language`, the user in Better Auth), how next-intl's proxy lives with the one of `@nano-app/frontend`, and the keys of the official components.

## Out of scope

Routes per locale (`/es/...`), translated content from the backend.

## Done when

The test app shows the login page in two languages, chosen by the user.
